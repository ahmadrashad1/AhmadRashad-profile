# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers evaluating Ahmad as a full-stack developer candidate. They land on the site cold, often on mobile, and decide within seconds whether to look closer. Secondary: potential freelance clients or collaborators who found him through GitHub and want a sense of who he is before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that makes a memorable first impression and drives visitors to two actions: look at real project work (his starred GitHub repos) and get in touch. Success looks like a recruiter remembering the site, clicking through to a project, and sending a message or email.

## Positioning

The dev portfolio that feels like you could actually reach out and press it — tactile, hand-shaped, unmistakably not another template.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, and quietly confident. The claymorphic surfaces invite touch and feel handmade, but the craft and restraint underneath signal competence, not novelty. Warm rather than corporate; precise rather than silly.

## Anti-references

Not a generic Wix/Squarespace portfolio template. Not flat glassmorphism or blur-heavy trend design. No gradient-text hero clichés, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, no cold enterprise-SaaS chrome.

## Design Principles

Every surface should look pressable, not just decorative — depth and softness communicate through shadow and form, not flat icons. Confidence through craft: the playful shapes stay polished and precise so the site reads as skilled work, not a novelty. Every screen has exactly one clear next step (contact or view work) — no competing CTAs. The starred repos do the talking about technical range instead of a wall of buzzwords or skill bars.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast throughout, including text sitting on clay-colored surfaces and shadows. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback for all hover/press/reveal animations. Keyboard-navigable CTAs and repo cards with visible focus states.
